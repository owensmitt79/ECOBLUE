Our Environmental & Waste Solutions

Four dedicated operational divisions delivering hygienic collection, circular recycling, environmental compliance, and heavy-duty logistics across Rivers State.

Division A

Waste Collection & Disposal

EcoBlue operates scheduled, disciplined waste evacuation services powered by our modern hydraulic compactor fleet. We ensure that residential areas, high-density estates, corporate campuses, and industrial premises remain spotless and sanitary.

Residential Waste Collection:

Direct curbside collection with scheduled morning routes across Port Harcourt neighborhoods.

Estate Waste Management:

Turnkey sanitation management for gated residential estates, homeowner associations, and housing complexes.

Commercial & Institutional Waste Management:

Structured evacuation contracts for shopping plazas, hotels, universities, and healthcare facilities.

Industrial Waste Evacuation:

Heavy-duty roll-on roll-off skip dispatch and solid non-hazardous waste evacuation for Trans-Amadi and manufacturing corridors.

Key Benefits
Predictable scheduled pickups • Hydraulic compaction preventing spillage • Elimination of perimeter odor • Full regulatory manifest reporting.
Division B

Recycling Services & Resource Recovery

Transforming municipal and commercial waste streams into valuable reusable raw commodities. Our recycling framework decreases landfill load while conserving energy and raw materials.

Plastic Recovery & Processing:

Segregating PET bottles, HDPE jugs, and polymers for baling, shredding, and industrial recycling supply.

Paper & Cardboard Recycling:

High-volume corrugated carton recovery from shopping centers, warehouses, and corporate offices.

Metal Recovery:

Ferrous and non-ferrous scrap sorting, separating aluminum beverage cans and steel components.

Waste Sorting & Resource Recovery:

Point-of-generation segregation bins and sorting support for zero-waste estate programs.

Visual Process

Our 5-Step Material Recovery Lifecycle

A closed-loop system ensuring every recoverable piece of waste is systematically categorized, converted, and returned to productive economic use.

1

Collection

Segregated pickup from residential estates, hotels, and commercial collection bins.

2

Sorting

Manual and mechanical segregation into plastics, cardboard, glass, and metal fractions.

3

Recovery

High-density baling, cleaning, and preparation of clean post-consumer commodity streams.

4

Processing

Flaking, granulation, or compaction ready for manufacturing raw material feedstocks.

5

Reuse

Reintegration into circular packaging, construction items, and sustainable manufacturing.

Division C

Environmental Services & Consultancy

Protecting public health and ecological biodiversity across Rivers State through deep sanitation interventions, neighborhood cleanups, and regulatory advisory.

Environmental Sanitation

Comprehensive deep-cleaning, surface decontamination, drain clearing, and municipal vector suppression.

Community Clean-ups

High-impact community mobilization campaigns transforming flooded or blocked drainage channels.

Environmental Consultancy

Waste audits, environmental management plans (EMP), and site compliance assessments.

Sustainability Advisory

Strategic roadmaps helping corporations reduce footprint and meet CAMA & state green guidelines.

Division D

Logistics & Support Services

Heavy-duty infrastructure, roll-on containers, mobile waste staging skips, and dedicated transportation fleets designed for heavy generators.

Waste Transportation & Evacuation:

Dedicated transport routes ensuring timely clearance with zero neighborhood downtime.

Fleet Management & GPS Tracking:

Real-time telematics tracking driver routes, compaction status, and turnaround times.

Bins & Skips Supply:

Lease and supply of standardized 240L/1100L heavy-duty wheeled bins and industrial metal skips.

Environmental Project Support:

Staging logistics for construction, dredging cleanup, oilfield non-hazardous site cleanouts.

Operational Fleet Infrastructure

GPS-Enabled Tracking

Operational Excellence

Driver & Handler Safety Standards

Our personnel receive continuous training on occupational safety, defensive driving in urban traffic, and sanitary handling procedures.

Mandatory PPE Standards

Every handler is outfitted with high-visibility reflective vests, steel-toe puncture-resistant boots, heavy puncture gloves, and respiratory protection.
