Practice Disciplines

Specialist Consulting Fields

We maintain an accredited database of external senior consultants and technical specialists across critical environmental domains in Nigeria.

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

Lead practitioners for baseline environmental sampling, biodiversity surveys, socio-economic impact studies, and statutory EIA documentation for Federal & Rivers State authorities.

Key Tools: Air quality samplers, toxicology profiling, ecological modeling.

Circular Economy & Polymer Engineering

Technical advisory on post-consumer plastic value chains (PET, HDPE, LDPE), polymer contamination reduction, extrusion technologies, and circular economy integration for industrial clients.

Key Tools: Material Flow Analysis (MFA), polymer purity testing, recycling yield metrics.

GIS Spatial Mapping & Route Telematics

Geographic Information System experts for waste generation density mapping, catchment zone optimization, and telematics-guided route planning for Port Harcourt compactor vehicles.

Key Tools: QGIS/ArcGIS, telemetry API analytics, municipal road network nodes.

Industrial Waste Audit & Remediation

Industrial hygiene, effluent testing, sludge decontamination, and waste minimization audits for oil and gas service bases, marine dockyards, and food manufacturing plants.

Key Tools: Chemical risk matrices, waste manifest protocols, bioremediation design.

Regulatory Compliance & Permitting

Specialists with verified liaison expertise in navigating regulatory frameworks under CAMA 2020, Rivers State Ministry of Environment (RSMEnv), RIWAMA, and FMEnv guidelines.

Key Tools: Legal compliance gap analysis, statutory permit registers, policy drafting.

Community Health & ESG Frameworks

Consultants specialized in designing community sanitation advocacy, informal waste picker integration frameworks, and measurable Corporate Environmental Social Governance (ESG) reports.

Key Tools: GRI reporting standards, community engagement metrics, social impact assessment.
