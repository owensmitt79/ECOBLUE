About Us

EcoBlue Environmental Services Ltd. is a science-aligned, technology-enabled environmental solutions and waste management company headquartered in Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

About EcoBlue

Our Story

EcoBlue Environmental Services Ltd. is a professional, sustainability-driven environmental services, waste management, and recycling enterprise delivering dependable collection, compliance assurance, and sustainable solutions across Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and Nigeria.

We support residential communities, commercial corporate facilities, industrial parks, and institutional clients through structured waste logistics, source segregation, and high-efficiency material recovery for plastics, cardboard, scrap metal, and non-hazardous recyclables.

Operating in strict accordance with the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020) and Rivers State environmental guidelines, EcoBlue delivers scheduled municipal routes, containerized skip placements, and site sanitations that protect urban neighborhoods and Niger Delta waterways from environmental degradation.
